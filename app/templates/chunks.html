Edit and save as many chunks as you like, then click the regenerate button once: only the chunks you touched are re-sent for generation (seconds, not minutes), and the chapter re-stitches automatically. Saving a chunk unchanged marks it for a re-roll — useful when the words are right but the read is off. To change a pause, edit its number; to remove it, save it empty.
