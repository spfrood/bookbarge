Voice tags Chatterbox understands ([sigh], [laugh], …)

Put a tag exactly where the sound should happen — it's performed as a real vocal reaction in the narrator's voice, not read aloud. Example: He looked at the bill. [sigh] "Fine."

TagWhat it does
[laugh]a full laugh
[chuckle]a short, soft laugh
[sigh]an audible sigh
[gasp]a sharp intake of breath
[groan]a groan
[cough]a cough
[clear throat]clears the throat
[sniff]a sniff
[shush]a hushing “shhh”

These nine are the documented set — any other bracketed text is read aloud as words, with two exceptions Bookbarge handles itself: [pause:2.4s] becomes exact silence, and misspelled pause markers are rejected at save time. Tags count toward the chunk character limit, and delivery is stochastic — if a tag gets skipped or overdone, save the chunk unchanged to re-roll it.
