Beta

Turn your book into an audiobook, narrated in a voice you choose.

Bookbarge converts a manuscript — uploaded as plain-text chapters plus a short voice sample — into a finished audiobook using AI voice cloning. You review every chapter by ear, re-generate anything that doesn't sound right, and download a complete audiobook with chapter markers.

Seats are limited during the beta. Anyone can register, but new accounts must be approved by an admin before they can log in — approval is manual and not instant, so expect a wait after signing up.
